## **1. Introduction**

> *"Beyond the moon, toward a token-powered galaxy."*

**Rocket Realms** is a Web3-native, skill-based arcade game where players launch NFT-powered rockets, navigate through dynamic space environments, and collect valuable loot to craft tools, upgrade gear, and earn on-chain rewards.

The game combines:

* **Hyper-addictive gameplay loops** (inspired by hits like Moonsheep Cannon)
* **Scarce digital ownership** via NFTs and tokenized propellant (\$NRG)
* **A sustainable economy** with token sinks, crafting, and a community-run marketplace

It’s designed for:

* **Players**: Fun, fair competition with real asset ownership
* **Investors**: Token utility, on-chain volume, and a self-sustaining ecosystem

---

## **2. Gameplay Overview**

### 🚀 Getting Started

To enter the Rocket Realms universe, players need:

1. A **Rocket NFT** — purchasable from the marketplace
2. A supply of **Propellant** (Solid, Liquid, or Nuclear)

Each rocket launch uses propellant and generates a run session where players explore, collect, and dodge hazards.

### 🎮 Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Launch**

   * Players click-to-launch a rocket
   * Trajectory and performance depend on rocket type, equipped tools, and propellant used

2. **Mid-Flight Navigation**

   * Use arrow keys (or swipe) to dodge meteors, airplanes, and satellites
   * Collect loot: 🧩 Rocket parts, 🔋 Propellant tanks, 🪙 \$NRG coins

3. **Endgame Choice**

   * **Crash?** Lose all collected loot
   * **Escape?** Safely recover a % of loot (VIPs keep 50% of propellant too)
   * **Low HP zone:** High risk, high reward — better loot, but more hazards

4. **Return & Progress**

   * Craft rare tools with loot
   * Sell tools or parts on marketplace
   * Reinvest \$NRG into factories, upgrades, or new rockets

## **3. Economy Design**

Rocket Realms is driven by a **player-owned economy** where every action has real value. The economic loop is centered around:

* **Rocket NFTs** (player access, rarity, performance)
* **Propellant** (fuel that enables gameplay)
* **Tools** (craftable, tradable, upgradeable gear)
* **\$NRG Token** (the core utility and reward currency)

### 🔄 Player Roles

| Role          | Strategy                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Launchers** | Focus on collecting loot and progressing through flight      |
| **Crafters**  | Specialize in combining parts into tools for resale          |
| **Traders**   | Arbitrage tools, propellant, or NFTs on the open marketplace |

These roles can evolve — one player might be a launcher one week, a crafter the next.

## **4. Tokenomics**

### 🪙 \$NRG Token

\$NRG is the **core utility token** of Rocket Realms. It is earned, spent, and recycled throughout the ecosystem.

#### ✅ \$NRG Use Cases

* **Factory fuel:** Required to run Liquid/Nuclear factories
* **Crafting:** Needed to craft or upgrade high-rarity tools
* **Marketplace fees**
* **Factory upgrades & VIP Pass**

#### 🔁 Token Sinks

To ensure sustainability, \$NRG is continuously burned via:

* Crafting failures
* Factory operation costs
* Marketplace royalties

#### 📈 Earning \$NRG

* Loot collection during flight
* Selling tools or parts to others
* Tournament rewards & ranking bonuses

### 💡 Example: Player Journey

1. Player launches using Solid Propellant
2. Collects 3x Rocket Parts and 1x \$NRG Tank
3. Uses 15 \$NRG to craft a **Speed Booster Tool**
4. Lists the item for 40 \$NRG on the marketplace
5. Uses profits to upgrade their factory for higher propellant limits

## **5. Propellant Factory System**

Propellant controls access to gameplay — creating a **daily economic loop**:

### 🔋 Solid Propellant

* Default for all players
* Daily production: 1,000 units
* Cost: Free
* Can launch **Common Rockets** only

### 💧 Liquid & 🔥 Nuclear Propellants

* Unlock by using **Factory Keys** (NFTs)
* Require \$NRG to operate
* Allow launch of higher-tier rockets

#### 🔑 Factory Keys

* Tradable NFTs
* Earned via:

  * Marketplace
  * Tournaments
  * 90-day login rewards
  * Monthly leaderboard

#### 🔼 Factory Upgrades

Players can spend \$NRG to upgrade:

* **Daily propellant production** (e.g., +150/day)
* **Storage capacity** (e.g., 300/3000 → 300/4000)

## **6. Crafting & Loot System**

Loot is the heartbeat of Rocket Realms’ progression. During each flight, players can collect items that can be **assembled into powerful tools** or **sold to others**.

### 🎁 Loot Types

* 🧩 **Tool Pieces** – Raw parts like:

  * Alloy Plate
  * Circuit Core
  * Fuel Nozzle
  * Coolant Tube
  * Plasma Cell
* 🔋 **Propellant Tanks** – Provides extra Solid, Liquid, or Nuclear fuel
* 🪙 **\$NRG Coins** – Small token drops

### 🧪 Crafting Tools

In the **Rocket Lab**, players can combine parts + \$NRG to craft tools:

| Tool Type             | Effect                               |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Speed Booster**     | Increases initial launch speed       |
| **Acceleration Chip** | Increases mid-flight acceleration    |
| **Upgraded Armor**    | -10% incoming damage                 |
| **Plasma Repair Kit** | Heals up to 30% rocket HP mid-flight |

Crafting high-rarity tools:

* Costs more \$NRG
* Has lower success rates (e.g., 60% → 35% → 10%)

### 🎲 Example: Crafting Plasma Booster

* Requires: 1x Plasma Cell, 2x Circuit Core, 3x Coolant Tube
* Crafting Cost: 50 \$NRG
* Success Rate: 25% (Legendary Tier)
* Failure = Loss of \$NRG + 1 random part

> Players must decide: sell the parts or risk crafting for greater profit?

## **7. NFT Mechanics**

### 🚀 Rocket NFTs

Every rocket is a unique on-chain NFT with the following attributes:

| Attribute           | Description                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Type**            | Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic  |
| **Propellant Type** | Which propellants it accepts           |
| **HP & Durability** | Affects time in-air and risk tolerance |
| **Upgrade Slots**   | Number of tools it can equip           |

### 🛠️ Tool NFTs

Tools can be:

* **Equipped** to enhance gameplay
* **Traded** as individual NFTs
* **Crafted** from loot during missions

### 🧬 Factory Keys (NFTs)

Factory keys grant access to higher-tier propellant factories:

* Liquid Factory Key NFT
* Nuclear Factory Key NFT

They are tradable on-chain assets with high strategic value.

### 🎨 Cosmetic Skins

Skins are optional NFT cosmetics:

* Do not affect gameplay
* Limited edition
* Tradable

## **8. Risk-Reward & Escape Mechanics**

Rocket Realms introduces **high-stakes decision making** mid-flight:

### ☠️ Crash vs Escape

* **Crash:** All loot is lost. End of run.
* **Escape:** Player recovers loot (and propellant if VIP).
* **Low HP (<10%)**: Unlocks **High-Risk Zone** with rare loot and greater danger

### ⚖️ Strategic Choice

Do you:

* Escape safely with moderate loot?
* Or risk it all in the danger zone for mythic-tier rewards?

These decisions fuel adrenaline and player stories — essential for content virality.

## **10. VIP Pass & Monetization**

### 💳 VIP Pass (Subscription NFT)

The **VIP Pass** is a time-limited NFT that grants premium benefits:

| Feature               | Free Player        | VIP Pass Holder         |
| --------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Propellant Efficiency | 1x                 | 1.5x gain on success    |
| Escape Recovery       | 0% propellant back | 50% propellant back     |
| Crafting Success      | Base rates         | +10% success chance     |
| Rocket Durability     | Normal             | -15% damage taken       |
| Marketplace Fees      | 5%                 | 1%                      |
| Early Access          | ❌                  | ✅ Priority new features |

> 🎫 VIP passes are minted on-chain and may be bought, sold, or rented via third-party protocols.

### 💰 Monetization Streams

Rocket Realms is built to sustain itself through balanced revenue sources:

1. **Primary Sales** – NFT Rockets, VIP Passes, Cosmetic Skins
2. **Secondary Market Royalties** – Rocket & tool trades
3. **\$NRG Usage** – Burning via crafting, fuel, upgrades
4. **Launch Fees** – Optional \$NRG launch boosters
5. **Lootbox System** *(optional future)* – Cosmetic lootboxes only

All game mechanics are designed to encourage **recirculation** of assets rather than extraction.
